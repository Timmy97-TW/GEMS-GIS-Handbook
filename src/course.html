The course課程

Ten classes with Dr. Daniel Bloom. The first half teaches QGIS on world and Taiwan data; the second half applies it to the project until every map in the storyline has a first version. Each class below lists what was taught, the links given out, the homework and the maps that came back. 與 Dr. Daniel Bloom 上的十堂課。前半段用全球與台灣資料教 QGIS；後半段套用到專案上，直到故事線裡的每張地圖都有第一版。以下每堂課列出教了什麼、發了哪些連結、作業內容，以及交回來的地圖。
