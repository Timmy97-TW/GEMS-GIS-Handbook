First Taiwan map第一張台灣地圖

Homework set on 2 July: one raster and four vector layers from the NLSC map server, with a title, north arrow and scale, exported as PDF.7 月 2 日的作業：從國土測繪中心圖資服務取一個網格圖層加四個向量圖層，加上標題、指北針、比例尺，輸出成 PDF。

Power plants in China中國發電廠分布

In-class exercise on 7 July with world data: renewable and non-renewable power plants, styled by category.7 月 7 日課堂練習，用全球資料把再生與非再生發電廠依類別上色。

Landslides and sensitive areas山崩與地質敏感區

Same day, Taiwan data: the Geological Survey's sensitive-area layers on the NLSC basemap, one region per person.同一天改用台灣資料：地調所地質敏感區圖層疊在國土測繪中心底圖上，每人負責一個區域。

Population maps人口地圖

9 July: each person mapped a country's population with graduated colours.7 月 9 日：每人選一個國家，用分級色彩畫人口分布。

Weather stations氣象測站

9 July: station coordinates from a CSV turned into points, then joined to 1991 to 2020 climate normals.7 月 9 日：把 CSV 的測站經緯度轉成點，再接上 1991 至 2020 年氣候平均值。

Crops, temperature and terrain作物、溫度與地形

20 to 28 July: the first maps that put two variables on one sheet.7 月 20 至 28 日：第一批把兩個變數放在同一張圖上的地圖。

Global warming in Taiwan台灣暖化

23 July: everyone made the same map from the same brief, which makes this set the best one for comparing design choices.7 月 23 日：同一份題目、每人各畫一張，最適合拿來比較設計選擇。

Warming animation暖化動畫

25 to 30 July: five versions of one animated map. The brief: four seconds at most, overlay the difference, let counties appear when their data starts.7 月 25 至 30 日：同一張動畫地圖的五個版本。要求：最多四秒、疊出差異、縣市在有資料的年份才出現。

Farmer age and heat stress農民年齡與熱逆境

29 July to 6 August: the demographic layer, and the overlay that became Figure 4 on the wiki.7 月 29 日至 8 月 6 日：人口結構圖層，以及後來成為 wiki 圖 4 的疊圖。
